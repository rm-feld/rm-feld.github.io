> [!attention] Stack Exchange Data Setting
> We consider the question-answer site [Stack Exchange (SE)](https://stackexchange.com). On SE, some users (who we will call **askers**) will pose a question that other users (who we will call **answerers**) can give answers to. Questions can receive no or many answers, and other users can upvote or downvote both the question and (some or all) answers. Askers are generally expected to tag questions with relevant keywords in order to encourage their question reaching the right experts. 
> %% %%
> By far the most active subset of SE is [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/questions).
