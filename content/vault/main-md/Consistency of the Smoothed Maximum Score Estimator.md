> [!tip] Consistency of the Smoothed Maximum Score Estimator
> **([[@horowitzSmoothedMaximumScore1992]], Theorem 1).** Let [[Standard Assumptions for Maximum Score Estimation|assumptions 1 -]] [[Consistency of the Maximum Score Estimator#^assumption-3-manski-consistency|3]] hold. Without loss of generality, let the first component be nontrivial, with $|\beta_{1}| = 1$, and let $\tilde{\beta} = (\beta_{2}, \dots, \beta _p)^{\intercal}$ be contained in a compact subset of $\mathbb{R}^{p - 1}$ (this is **Assumption 4**). Then the [[Smoothed Maximum Score Estimator]] converges to its true value $\beta$ almost surely.
