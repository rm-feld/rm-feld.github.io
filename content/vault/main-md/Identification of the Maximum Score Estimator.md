> [!tip] Identification of the Maximum Score Estimator 
> **Lemma 2, [[@manskiSemiparametricAnalysisDiscrete1985]].** Let us satisfy the [[Standard Assumptions for Maximum Score Estimation|given assumptions for maximum score estimation]]. Then $\beta$ is [[Linear Median-Discrete Response (LMDR) Identification|LMDR-identified]]. 

%% %%

> [!abstract]- Proof Sketch
> It can be shown that if $\beta \neq \beta_{0}$, then there exists a sign relation that will not necessarily coincide with $\beta_{0}$, ie. $\mathbb{P}\left( x^{\intercal}\beta < 0 < x^{\intercal}\beta_{0} \right) > 0$ (and/or the opposite sign relation). This of course gives non-trivial weight to the integral $R(\beta)$. Manski's proof does case work on the sign of components of $\beta$.
